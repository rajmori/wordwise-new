Serendipity

/ˌser.ənˈdɪp.ə.ti/
noun

Definition

The occurrence and development of events by chance in a happy or beneficial way.

🧠 Memory Hook

Think of a "Serene Dip" in a pool where you find a treasure chest floating by—a happy accident!

📖 Context

"The discovery of penicillin was a stroke of serendipity; Fleming noticed the mold killing bacteria by pure chance."

🌱 Roots & Origins

Coined by Horace Walpole from the Persian fairy tale The Three Princes of Serendip, whose heroes were always making discoveries by accidents and sagacity.

Ephemeral

/əˈfem.ər.əl/
adjective

Definition

Lasting for a very short time; short-lived; transitory.

📖 Context

"The ephemeral beauty of a sunset reminds us to cherish the moment before it fades into the night."

🧠 Memory Hook

⏳

Think "E-fem-eral" → "Fem" sounds like "Fame".
Fame is often short-lived and fleeting.

🌱 Roots

  • epi- (Greek): on, upon
  • hemera (Greek): day
  • Literally: "lasting only one day"
